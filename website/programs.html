Ages 1–3

Toddler community

Big steps for our youngest.

Toddlers want to do everything themselves, and our toddler room is built to let them. A wooden climber and slide, an alphabet rug and open floor space invite movement, while low shelves hold just a few activities at a time.

Days centre on Practical Life: pouring, carrying, tidying, dressing and caring for their own things. These first habits of independence and concentration are the foundation for everything that follows in the primary classroom.

Primary classroom

Three years, five areas, one community.

Our primary classrooms bring together children from three to six. The Montessori Classrooms are grouped into five general areas: Practical Life, Sensorial, Language, Math and Science. The children are able to make independent choices in areas that are of greatest interest to them.

The emphasis is on individualized learning, with daily opportunities for group projects and social interaction. Older children become the classroom’s natural leaders, and younger children learn by watching them.

Voluntary Pre-Kindergarten

We are a Florida VPK provider.

Florida’s Voluntary Prekindergarten program gives every eligible four-year-old free pre-kindergarten hours to prepare for kindergarten. At Bear Lake, those hours happen inside a Montessori classroom, alongside the rest of the primary community.

Apply for your child’s Certificate of Eligibility online, then bring it to us when you enroll. Families who need a longer day can add wraparound care before and after the VPK hours.
