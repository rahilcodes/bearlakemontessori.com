The Montessori guide

Three things our teachers do all day.

  1. 1

    Prepare the environment. Shelves are arranged, materials are complete and in good order, and everything a child needs is within reach.

  2. 2

    Observe. Teachers watch closely to understand each child’s interests and readiness, as Dr. Montessori did in her first classroom.

  3. 3

    Present, then withdraw. A child is shown how to follow through their choice. Once the activity is mastered, the teacher steps back.
