Questions, answered.

Everything parents most often ask us, in one place. Search below or pick a topic. If your question is not here, call (407) 786-2255.

1The school

What ages do you serve?

We welcome children from 2 to 6 years old, in multi-age Montessori classrooms.

Where is Bear Lake Montessori Academy?

We are at 843 N SR 434, Altamonte Springs, FL 32714. The school recently moved to this location, which has been extensively re-developed for young children. See the map and directions.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 7:15am to 6:00pm. The school is closed on weekends.

Is the school licensed?

Yes. Bear Lake Montessori Academy is a child care facility licensed by the Florida Department of Children and Families, license number C18SE0374, with a licensed capacity of 50 children.

How do I stay up to date with my child’s day?

Parents receive updates and photos of the activities their children do during the day. You can also follow the school on Instagram at @bearlakemontessori.

Is there outdoor play?

Yes. Our new, fully fenced playground has soft turf, slides and a climbing frame under a shade canopy, and the covered porch beside the classrooms is used for outdoor work.

2Programs & VPK

Do you offer full-day and half-day programs?

Yes. We offer both full-day and half-day schedules, as well as before school and after school care within our 7:15am to 6:00pm hours.

Are you a Florida VPK provider?

Yes. Florida’s Voluntary Prekindergarten (VPK) program provides free pre-kindergarten hours for four-year-olds who live in Florida. At Bear Lake, VPK children learn in our Montessori primary classroom.

Who is eligible for VPK, and how do I apply?

Children must live in Florida and be four years old on or before September 1 of the school year. Apply for a Certificate of Eligibility through Florida’s Family Portal at familyservices.floridaearlylearning.com, then bring it to us when you enroll.

Can I add care around VPK hours?

Yes. Families who need a longer day can combine VPK hours with before and after school care. Ask us which combinations are available for your child.

3Admissions

How do I book a tour?

Tours are booked by phone or email. Call (407) 786-2255, email bearlakemontessori@gmail.com, or use the tour request form.

How much is tuition?

Tuition depends on your child’s age and schedule, so we share it with families directly. Call us or ask during your tour. VPK hours are free for eligible four-year-olds.

Do you have openings right now?

We are currently enrolling children ages 2 to 6. Availability changes through the year, so contact us to check openings for your child’s age and preferred schedule.

What paperwork is needed to enroll?

Florida requires every child in licensed care to have:

  • A Student Health Examination (form DH 3040)
  • A Florida Certification of Immunization (form DH 680), or a valid exemption

You will also complete our enrollment forms, and bring a VPK Certificate of Eligibility if applicable.

4Montessori

What is Montessori?

Montessori is a philosophy with the fundamental tenet that a child learns best within a social environment which supports each individual’s unique development. It was created by Dr. Maria Montessori, who opened her first “Children’s House” in Rome in 1907. Read about our approach.

Why are different ages in one classroom?

A multi-aged grouping is one of the hallmarks of a Montessori classroom. Younger children learn by watching older ones, and older children strengthen their own understanding by helping. It encourages cooperative learning, peer teaching and emotional development.

If children choose their work, how do they learn everything?

Children make independent choices in the areas that interest them most, and are then shown how to follow through their choice. Teachers observe each child and introduce new materials when they are ready, so every area of the curriculum is covered over time.

What will my child learn?

Our classrooms are grouped into five areas: Practical Life, Sensorial, Language, Mathematics, and Science & Culture. Explore the curriculum.
