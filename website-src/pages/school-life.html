Home/School life

A day at Bear Lake

Early care opens at 7:30, the school day begins at 8:00, and the last goodbyes are said by 6:00. In between, children work, play, talk, eat, build friendships and spend time outdoors, all at a pace that respects them.

The daily rhythm

The shape of a Montessori day.

Montessori days follow a calm, predictable rhythm, built around a long, uninterrupted period of work in the morning. Predictability helps young children feel secure enough to explore.

Timings vary a little by age group; we will walk you through your child’s day on your tour.

  1. 1

    Morning arrival & greeting

    Early care from 7:30, school from 8:00. Coats and bags go in the cubby, and each child is greeted by name.

  2. 2

    Uninterrupted work time

    The heart of the day: a long, calm stretch with the Montessori materials, choosing work across the five areas.

  3. 3

    Group circle

    The class gathers for the calendar, songs, stories and conversation.

  4. 4

    Outdoor play

    Running, climbing and sliding on the new playground, or working on the covered porch.

  5. 5

    Lunch

    Children bring their own packed lunch from home and eat together.

  6. 6

    Rest

    A quiet rest to recharge after a busy morning.

  7. 7

    Afternoon enrichment

    Afternoon activities until families collect their children, by 6:00.

Everyday moments

Small rituals, big learning.

A few of the things you will find children doing around the school on any given day.

The calendar

Our wooden perpetual calendar tracks yesterday, today and tomorrow, the month, the season and the phases of the moon.

Dolls of the world

Traditional dress from around the globe sparks conversations about geography and culture.

Art we make

Paper flowers, collages and paintings made by the children decorate our walls.

Quiet reading

A cosy corner with child-sized armchairs for looking at books alone or with a friend.

Every day

Group projects

The emphasis is on individualized learning, but there are daily opportunities for group projects and social interactions. The spirit of respect, love and cooperation is developed.

Outdoors

A brand new playground.
