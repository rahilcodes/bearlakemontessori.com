Our home

New location, new premises, same high quality schooling.

Bear Lake Montessori Academy is situated in the city of Altamonte Springs. Our new location has been re-developed extensively to provide a safe environment for our young students, whilst ensuring they have the space to learn and grow.

Inside, the classrooms are bright and open, with large windows, low wooden shelves and child-sized tables and chairs. Outside there is a covered porch for working in the fresh air and a fenced, shaded playground. We are proud of our school and encourage you to come and see it for yourself.

What we stand for

Respect. Freedom. Independence.

Three words that have guided Bear Lake from the start. Each one shows up in the classroom every day.

R

Respect

Children are respected as different from adults and as individuals who are different from one another. Teachers follow each child’s pace and interests instead of moving the whole class in step.

F

Freedom

Children are able to make independent choices in the areas that are of greatest interest to them. They are then shown how to follow through their choice, so freedom comes with focus.

I

Independence

Practical Life exercises help children care for themselves and their environment. Once an activity is mastered, the teacher steps back and the child works on their own.
