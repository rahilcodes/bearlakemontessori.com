What to bring

Paperwork, made simple.

As a child care facility licensed by the Florida Department of Children and Families, we keep a few required records for every child. Your pediatrician can provide the two state forms.

  • Student Health Examination (Florida form DH 3040), completed by your child’s doctor.
  • Florida Certification of Immunization (form DH 680), or a valid exemption.
  • Our enrollment packet, with emergency contacts and authorized pick-up adults.
  • VPK Certificate of Eligibility, if your four-year-old will attend VPK hours.

Admissions questions

All FAQs →
Can my child start part way through the year?

Availability changes throughout the year. Call {{PHONE}} or email {{EMAIL}} and we will tell you which schedules currently have openings for your child’s age.

Do I need to tour before enrolling?

We strongly encourage it. The best way to understand a Montessori classroom is to stand in one, and a visit lets you ask questions and see whether Bear Lake is right for your family.

How do I apply for VPK?

Apply for a VPK Certificate of Eligibility through Florida’s Family Portal at familyservices.floridaearlylearning.com, then bring the certificate to us when you enroll. Children must be four years old on or before September 1 of the school year.
