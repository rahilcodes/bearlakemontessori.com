The Montessori way

Children learn best when they’re free to explore.

Three ideas sit underneath everything we do in the classroom.

1

Montessori Classroom

A Montessori classroom provides an atmosphere which encourages social interaction for cooperative learning, peer teaching and emotional development.

2

Origins

Montessori is a philosophy with the fundamental tenet that a child learns best within a social environment which supports each individual’s unique development.

3

Curriculum

The Montessori Classrooms are grouped into five general areas: Practical Life, Sensorial, Language, Math and Science. The children are able to make independent choices in areas that are of greatest interest to them.

The curriculum

Five areas to wander into.

The children are able to make independent choices in areas that are of greatest interest to them. They are then shown how to follow through their choice. Once the activity is mastered, the teacher can withdraw from the activity and the child will be able to work independently.
